Club Shop

Butterfly equipment, apparel, and expert help

Our club shop offers genuine Butterfly equipment at competitive prices, with guidance from experienced staff to help you find the right setup.

We carry a range of table tennis gear for players of all levels, including:

  • Pre-made rackets, blades, and rubbers
  • Shoes, shirts, and other apparel
  • Accessories and training essentials
  • Professional help choosing equipment that fits your playing style

If we do not currently have a Butterfly product in stock, we can help order it for you.
