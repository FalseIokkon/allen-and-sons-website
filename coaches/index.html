Coaches

National champions, ITTF-certified coaches, and decades of experience — available for players of every level.

Take a Lesson Book a Session

Fovad Kaseb

Club Owner
  • 1985, 1988, 1989 — Iranian National Champion.
  • 1997 — West Asian Games Champion.
  • 2000-2010 — Head Coach of the Iranian National Men's Table Tennis Team.
  • Certified ITTF High Performance Coach.
  • Certified US National Coach.
  • 2003-2010 — ATTU full member of Junior & Coaching Committee.

Dinh Nguyen

Style · Shakehands looper

  • Former leader of the Vietnamese Junior National Team.
  • Created a system for teaching beginner-level players.
  • Over 22 years of coaching experience in the U.S.

Bella Livshin

  • Coaching since 1968, making her our most experienced coach.
  • Won numerous US Open and US National titles in over-40, over-50, and over-60 groups.
  • Available to train players of all skill levels.

Vahid Mosaferi

Style · Shakehands looper

  • International Table Tennis Federation (ITTF) Coach.
  • Over 20 years of international experience as a player and coach.
  • Iranian National Team player.
  • Played in multiple professional leagues.

Kim Gilbert

  • Kim is a professional table tennis champion and elite coach.
  • She is a three-time U.S. Olympic Festival Gold Medalist.
  • She currently holds the Olympic Record for the fastest reflexes ever recorded by any man or woman in any sport.
  • Kim is also the CEO of Pongstarz which produces and manages large scale ping pong themed events and celebrity fundraisers.

Rudy Miranda

Style · Two wing looper

  • Freelance table tennis coach for more than 20 years.
  • Competed Nationally and Internationally.
  • Was a member of the Nicaraguan International Team.
  • Participated in 6 world championships, Germany (twice), China, Japan, Russia, and France.
  • ITTF Certified Coach.
